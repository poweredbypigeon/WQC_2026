# Q5

Plese implement the Liar's Poker probability calculator.

- Context: there are 54 cards (13 ranks x 4 suits, plus 2 jokers which can serve as any card), there are 3 face up cards and 2 face down cards. 
Compute the probability of each of these hands: 

1. Six-of-a-Kind: Six cards of the same rank.
2. Five-of-a-Kind: Five cards of the same rank.
3. Straight Flush: Five consecutive cards of the same suit. Aces can act as the highest or
lowest card, so both (A, 2, 3, 4, 5) and (10, J, Q, K, A) are considered consecutive.
4. Four-of-a-Kind: Four cards of the same rank.
5. Full House: Three cards of the same rank, plus a pair of a different rank.
6. Three-of-a-Kind: Three cards of the same rank.
7. Two Pair: Two pairs of different ranks.
8. Straight: Five consecutive cards.
9. Pair: Two cards of the same rank.
10. High Card: A single card of any rank.

- Additional stipulation: assume the rank and suit are specified, and then out of all combinations (e.g. pair of 6s, pair of 7s, etc.) pick the highest probability hand. 

- Input should be in the form of 3 cards. (e.g. "2HJD8C" would mean 2 of hearts, jack of diamonds, and 8 of clubs). The program should then calculate the probabilities of each hand based on the given input.
